# Ethical Considerations & Clinical Disclaimers | الأخلاقيات وإخلاء المسؤولية

1. **Intended Use**: Daleel is an academic research graduation project designed for literature discovery and synthesis assistance. It is NOT a certified medical diagnostic device.
2. **Clinical Decision Support**: All synthetic answers must clearly reference peer-reviewed literature. No recommendations should be taken as direct medical advice without clinician review.
3. **Data Privacy**: No patient identifiable data (PHI) is processed or stored. All corpora are derived from publicly accessible databases (PubMed, PMC OA subset, public clinical guidelines).
